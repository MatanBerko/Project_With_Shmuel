Draft caption, Fig. 6 (figures/fig_turbulence.pdf)

Turbulence two ways, in 4 pc bins of signed z over the 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint. Colour marks the phase (CNM, WNM in phase colours; all neutral gas thin black, from the weight only) and line style the method; the dotted vertical line marks z_mid. Solid: from the weight (alpha), assigning all of the missing support alpha - 1 to motions. Dashed: from the density-PDF width, using the volume-weighted standard deviation sigma_s of s = ln(n/<n>) over the cells of each phase in the bin (<n> the volume mean) and the turbulent relation M3D^2 = (exp(sigma_s^2) - 1) / b^2, drawn for b = 0.4; the hatched band spans b = 1 (compressive forcing, lowest) to b = 1/3 (solenoidal, highest). Both methods use the 1D convention of Fig. 7, M^2 = sigma_1D^2 / c_s^2 with c_s^2 = sum P_th / sum rho (P_th = 1.1 n_H k T, rho = 1.4 m_H n_H); the PDF Mach number is made 1D by isotropy, M^2 = M3D^2 / 3. (a) The 1D velocities: the non-thermal dispersion sigma_nt = (W/<rho> - c_s^2)^1/2 from the weight, sigma_turb = M c_s from the PDF width, and the mass-weighted sound speed c_s shared by both methods (thin dotted lines in the phase colours). (b) M^2: the mass-weighted mean of the per-cell alpha_cell - 1 from the weight, and M3D^2 / 3 from the PDF width. Filled bands are the mass-weighted 15-85% ranges of the per-cell quantities (max(W - P_th, 0)/rho)^1/2 and W/P_th - 1 for the CNM and WNM; beyond z_mid - 160 pc and z_mid + 220 pc more than 15% of the WNM mass has alpha_cell < 1, so the WNM band in (a) reaches zero there. Each phase is drawn only over the contiguous range from z_mid where it has at least 100 cells and 1% of the bin volume. The two methods bracket the turbulence from opposite sides: the PDF width is a LOWER limit, because the map's resolution and regularisation remove small-scale density variance (and the density-based phase classification truncates each phase's density distribution), while the weight gives an UPPER limit, because magnetic and cosmic-ray pressure also contribute to alpha - 1. Block-averaging the density to 4 pc lowers sigma_s by only 1-3% (appendix figure): the map holds almost no variance between 2 and 4 pc, so its effective resolution is coarser than its grid and the missing variance lies on scales the map does not resolve. In the midplane slab (|z - z_mid| <= 30 pc), the PDF width gives sigma_s = 0.50 and 0.62 for the CNM and WNM, i.e. M^2 = 0.60 (b = 1 to 1/3: 0.10-0.86) and 0.97 (0.16-1.39) and sigma_turb = 0.8 km/s (0.3-1.0) and 7.0 km/s (2.8-8.4); the weight gives, for the CNM, WNM and all neutral gas, <M^2> = 4.0 (15-85%: 1.7-6.6), 1.2 (0.1-2.4) and 2.7, and sigma_nt = 2.1 km/s (1.2-2.7), 8.1 km/s (2.5-11.5) and 4.7 km/s; c_s = 1.0, 7.1 and 4.1 km/s. For both phases the PDF estimate lies below the weight estimate, as the bracket requires: by a factor ~7 in M^2 for the CNM, and only ~1.3 for the WNM. The density-PDF width of all neutral gas is not drawn: it mixes the CNM and WNM, whose densities differ by ~two orders of magnitude, so it measures the CNM/WNM phase contrast rather than turbulent compression, and is shown only in the appendix figure (fig_density_pdf_width).
