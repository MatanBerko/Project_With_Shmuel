Draft caption, Fig. 6 (figures/fig_turbulence.pdf)

Turbulence two ways, in 4 pc bins of signed z over the 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint. Colour marks the phase (CNM, WNM in phase colours; all neutral gas thin black) and line style the method; the dotted vertical line marks z_mid. Solid: from the weight (alpha), assigning all of the missing support alpha - 1 to motions. Dashed: from the density-PDF width, using the volume-weighted standard deviation sigma_s of s = ln(n/<n>) over the cells of each phase in the bin (<n> the volume mean) and the turbulent relation M3D^2 = (exp(sigma_s^2) - 1) / b^2, drawn for b = 0.4; the hatched band spans b = 1 (compressive forcing, lowest) to b = 1/3 (solenoidal, highest). Both methods use the 1D convention of Fig. 7, M^2 = sigma_1D^2 / c_s^2 with c_s^2 = sum P_th / sum rho (P_th = 1.1 n_H k T, rho = 1.4 m_H n_H); the PDF Mach number is made 1D by isotropy, M^2 = M3D^2 / 3. (a) The non-thermal 1D velocity: sigma_nt = (W/<rho> - c_s^2)^1/2 from the weight, and sigma_turb = M c_s from the PDF width; (b) the mass-weighted sound speed c_s shared by both methods (as Fig. 7b), on the same axis range as (a); (c) M^2: the mass-weighted mean of the per-cell alpha_cell - 1 from the weight, and M3D^2 / 3 from the PDF width. Filled bands are the mass-weighted 15-85% ranges of the per-cell quantities (max(W - P_th, 0)/rho)^1/2, (P_th/rho)^1/2 and W/P_th - 1 for the CNM and WNM; beyond z_mid - 160 pc and z_mid + 220 pc more than 15% of the WNM mass has alpha_cell < 1, so the lower edge of the WNM band in (a) is zero and runs off the axis. Each phase is drawn only over the contiguous range from z_mid where it has at least 100 cells and 1% of the bin volume. The two methods bracket the turbulence from opposite sides: the PDF width is a LOWER limit, because the map's resolution and regularisation remove small-scale density variance (and the density-based phase classification truncates each phase's density distribution), while the weight gives an UPPER limit, because magnetic and cosmic-ray pressure also contribute to alpha - 1. Block-averaging the density to 4 pc lowers sigma_s by only 1-2% (appendix figure), so the missing variance lies below the map's effective resolution, not between 2 and 4 pc. In the midplane slab (|z - z_mid| <= 30 pc), the PDF width gives sigma_s = 0.50, 0.62 and 1.07 for the CNM, WNM and all neutral gas, i.e. M^2 = 0.60 (b = 1 to 1/3: 0.10-0.86), 0.97 (0.16-1.39) and 4.5 (0.7-6.4) and sigma_turb = 0.8 km/s (0.3-1.0), 7.0 km/s (2.8-8.4) and 8.6 km/s (3.5-10.4); the weight gives <M^2> = 4.0 (15-85%: 1.7-6.6), 1.2 (0.1-2.4) and 2.7, and sigma_nt = 2.1 km/s (1.2-2.7), 8.1 km/s (2.5-11.5) and 4.7 km/s. For the CNM and WNM the PDF estimate lies below the weight estimate, as the bracket requires: by a factor ~7 in M^2 for the CNM, and only ~1.3 for the WNM. For all neutral gas it does not: the neutral density distribution mixes two thermal phases whose densities differ by ~two orders of magnitude, so its width measures the phase contrast of the thermally bistable medium rather than turbulent compression, and the dashed black lines are not a turbulence estimate.
