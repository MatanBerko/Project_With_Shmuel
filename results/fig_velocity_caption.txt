Draft caption, Fig. 7 (figures/fig_velocity.pdf)

The missing vertical support expressed as a velocity, in 4 pc bins of signed z over the 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint; the dotted vertical line marks z_mid. (a) The effective dispersion sigma_eff = (W / <rho>)^1/2 that would hold the gas up against its area-averaged weight W, for all gas (thick black), the CNM and the WNM (solid), together with the mass-weighted isothermal thermal dispersion sigma_th = (sum P_th / sum rho)^1/2 of each phase (dashed); shaded bands are the WNM line-of-sight total (8.0 +- 0.6 km/s) and thermal (7.0 +- 0.7 km/s) dispersions of Marchal & Miville-Deschenes (2021). (b) The mass-weighted mean of the per-cell M^2 = W/P_th - 1 = alpha_cell - 1 for the CNM, the WNM and all neutral gas (thin black), with cells at alpha_cell < 1 kept as negative contributions; the dotted line is the CNM value of Heiles & Troland (2003), M_t ~ 3 converted to 1D as M_t^2/3 = 3.0, and the band is the WNM value of Marchal & Miville-Deschenes (2021), Ms = 0.87 +- 0.15 converted from 3D adiabatic to 1D isothermal as Ms^2 (5/3)/3 = 0.29-0.58. Every phase in a bin feels the same W, all velocities are 1D with c_s^2 = P_th/rho (P_th = 1.1 n_H k T, rho = 1.4 m_H n_H), and each phase is drawn only over the contiguous range from z_mid where it has at least 100 cells and 1% of the bin volume. Because all of alpha - 1 is assigned to motions, these are upper limits on the turbulent velocities: magnetic and cosmic-ray pressure also contribute to the support. In the midplane slab (|z - z_mid| <= 30 pc), sigma_eff = 8.3 km/s for all gas, 2.3 km/s (sigma_th = 1.0 km/s) for the CNM and 10.8 km/s (sigma_th = 7.1 km/s) for the WNM, and <M^2> = 4.0 (CNM), 1.2 (WNM) and 2.7 (all neutral gas).
