Draft caption, Fig. 7 (figures/fig_velocity.pdf)

The missing vertical support expressed as a velocity, in 4 pc bins of signed z over the 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint, for the CNM and WNM (solid, phase colours) and all neutral gas (thin black); the dotted vertical line marks z_mid. (a) The effective dispersion sigma_eff = (W / <rho>)^1/2 that would hold each phase up against the area-averaged gas weight W of its height bin; (b) the mass-weighted isothermal sound speed c_s = (sum P_th / sum rho)^1/2, on the same axis range as (a); (c) the mass-weighted mean of the per-cell M^2 = W/P_th - 1 = alpha_cell - 1, with cells at alpha_cell < 1 kept as negative contributions. Shaded bands are the mass-weighted 15-85% ranges of the per-cell quantities (W/rho)^1/2, (P_th/rho)^1/2 and W/P_th - 1, for the CNM and WNM only; each phase (line and band) is drawn only over the contiguous range from z_mid where it has at least 100 cells and 1% of the bin volume. Every phase in a bin feels the same W, and all velocities are 1D with c_s^2 = P_th/rho (P_th = 1.1 n_H k T, rho = 1.4 m_H n_H). Because all of alpha - 1 is assigned to motions, these are upper limits on the turbulent velocities: magnetic and cosmic-ray pressure also contribute to the support. In the midplane slab (|z - z_mid| <= 30 pc), sigma_eff = 2.3 km/s (15-85%: 1.5-3.0) for the CNM, 10.8 km/s (7.3-13.6) for the WNM and 6.2 km/s for all neutral gas; c_s = 1.0 km/s (0.8-1.2), 7.1 km/s (6.8-7.4) and 4.1 km/s; and <M^2> = 4.0 (1.7-6.6), 1.2 (0.1-2.4) and 2.7. For comparison, Heiles & Troland (2003) find M_t ~ 3 for CNM components (3D, isothermal sound speed), with M_t increasing with T_s; at the CNM temperatures found here (120-240 K) their fit (their Eq. 26) gives M^2 = 3.4-4.5 in our 1D convention, M^2 = M_t^2 / 3.3. Marchal & Miville-Deschenes (2021) find, for the WNM toward the North Ecliptic Pole at the 130 pc scale, a turbulent line-of-sight dispersion of 3.8 +- 0.4 km/s, T_k = 6000 +- 1300 K and Ms = 0.87 +- 0.15 (3D, adiabatic sound speed), i.e. M^2 = Ms^2 / 1.98 = 0.26-0.53 in our convention. Their 8.0 and 7.0 km/s total and thermal widths are Doppler widths of hydrogen atoms, sqrt(kT/m_H), and are not compared with c_s.
