Figure 4. Vertical profiles of the gas weight and the thermal pressure and of their ratio alpha, in
4 pc bins of height z over the 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint (RAW density, footprint-mean self-gravity,
balanced midplane z_mid = -11.9 pc, marked by the dotted line). (a) The area-averaged gas weight <W>,
the plain mean of the per-column hydrostatic weight W (the integral of rho g from z to the top of the
column) over all cells of the bin (HIM-flagged cells included), and the harmonic-mean thermal
pressure <1/P_th>^-1 over the non-HIM cells, with the shaded band spanning the 15th-85th percentiles
of the cells' P_th. (b) The headline <alpha_cell> = <W> <1/P_th> (thick line), the median of the
per-cell alpha_cell = <W>/P_th (dashed) and its 15th-85th percentile range (shaded); the grey line
marks alpha = 1. All statistics are volume-weighted (equal weight per cell). In vertical dynamical
equilibrium the weight equals the total midplane pressure, W = P_tot, so alpha measures P_tot/P_th.
In the midplane slab |z - z_mid| <= 30 pc, <W> = 7826 K cm^-3, <1/P_th>^-1 = 2406 K cm^-3,
<alpha_cell> = 3.25, and the median alpha_cell is 2.71 (15-85%: 1.46-5.39).
