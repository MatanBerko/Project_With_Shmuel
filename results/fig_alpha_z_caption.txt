Figure 4. Vertical profiles of the total and thermal pressure and of their ratio alpha, in 4 pc bins
of height z over the 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint (RAW density, footprint-mean self-gravity, balanced
midplane z_mid = -11.9 pc, marked by the dotted line). (a) The area-averaged total pressure <P_tot>,
the plain mean of the per-column hydrostatic P_tot over all cells of the bin (HIM-flagged cells
included), and the harmonic-mean thermal pressure <1/P_th>^-1 over the non-HIM cells, with the shaded
band spanning the 15th-85th percentiles of the cells' P_th. (b) The headline <alpha_cell> =
<P_tot> <1/P_th> (thick line), the median of the per-cell alpha_cell = <P_tot>/P_th (dashed) and
its 15th-85th percentile range (shaded); the grey line marks alpha = 1. All statistics are
volume-weighted (equal weight per cell). In the midplane slab |z - z_mid| <= 30 pc, <P_tot> = 7826
K cm^-3, <1/P_th>^-1 = 2406 K cm^-3, <alpha_cell> = 3.25, and the median alpha_cell is 2.71
(15-85%: 1.46-5.39).
