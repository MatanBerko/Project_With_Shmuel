Draft caption, Fig. 5 (figures/fig_phase_pdfs.pdf)

Density distributions of the neutral phases at four heights, over the 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint. Panels show the CNM (a), UNM (b) and WNM (c), each as step lines of the volume-weighted distribution of log10(n_H / cm^-3) in 0.05 dex bins, one line per slab in shades of the phase colour: darkest for the midplane slab, lighter with height. Slabs are |z - z_c| <= 30 pc: at d = 0 the single slab centred on z_mid, and at d = 100, 200 and 300 pc the union of the slabs centred on z_mid - d and z_mid + d (above and below combined). Each curve is the cell count per bin divided by the total number of cells in the slab (HIM included) and by the bin width in dex, so the area under a curve is that phase's volume fraction in the slab; curves of one panel can therefore be compared directly, and the three panels sum, together with the HIM, to unity. All cells are 2 pc cubes, so cell counts are volumes. Phases are from the dP/dn classification used in Figs. 6 and 7 (Shelest et al. 2026): with I_UV the local radiation field, WNM is n_H < n_W,max(I_UV) and CNM is n_H > n_C,min(I_UV), the densities of the warm- and cold-branch turning points (dP/dn = 0) of the thermal-equilibrium curve, and UNM lies between them. Because the phases are defined by density thresholds, each distribution has sharp edges at the thresholds; the edges are not perfectly vertical because the thresholds are evaluated at each cell's own I_UV. Hot ionised cells (HIM) are flagged separately and their distributions are not drawn, as their n_H is not physical; their volume fractions are 0.453, 0.589, 0.723 and 0.840 at d = 0, 100, 200 and 300 pc. The CNM, UNM and WNM volume fractions are 0.019, 0.173 and 0.354 at d = 0; 0.009, 0.075 and 0.327 at d = 100 pc; 0.0018, 0.025 and 0.250 at d = 200 pc; and 0.0002, 0.0095 and 0.150 at d = 300 pc. The volume-weighted median log10 n_H (16-84% range; read from the 0.05 dex bins) is 0.83 (0.68-1.08), 0.23 (-0.03-0.48) and -0.48 (-0.78 - -0.18) for the CNM, UNM and WNM at d = 0, and 0.83 (0.73-1.03), 0.03 (-0.08-0.28) and -0.88 (-1.08 - -0.53) at d = 300 pc. Every phase has at least 100 cells in every slab, so no curve is omitted.
