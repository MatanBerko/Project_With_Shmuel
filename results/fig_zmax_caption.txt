Figure. The footprint-mean gas density, the quality of single-layer fits and the midplane alpha
as functions of height, within the cube (|z| <= 750 pc). RAW density, all footprint cells
(HIM-flagged included), |x|, |y| <= 500 pc, balanced midplane z_mid = -11.9 pc (dotted line).
(a) <n_H>_area in 4 pc bins of u = |z - z_mid|, upper (z > z_mid) and lower (z < z_mid) sides
separately, plotted at signed z. The band is the error of log10 <n_H>_area: the standard
deviation of log10 <n_H> over the 16 disjoint 250 x 250 pc squares of the footprint divided by
sqrt(16). Coloured curves: exponential n0 exp(-u/h), Gaussian n0 exp(-u^2/2h^2) and sech^2
n0 sech^2(u/h) layers centred on z_mid, with free n0 and h, fitted to the binned log10 density of
each side by least squares with these errors. Each curve is the best fit over the full range,
all bins with upper edge <= 750 pc (u <= 748 pc on the upper side; u <= 738 pc on the lower side,
where the cube ends), drawn over the fitted bins. (b) Reduced chi^2 = chi^2/(N_bins - 2) of the
same fits versus the fit range z_max (all bins with upper edge <= z_max), z_max = 100-750 pc;
solid for the upper side, dashed for the lower side. (c) The headline midplane
<alpha_cell> = <W> <1/P_th> over the slab |z - z_mid| <= 30 pc (as in Fig. 4) versus the
integration height z_top: the density is set to zero at |z| > z_top, and the gas self-gravity,
the balanced midplane and the weight W are recomputed from the truncated profile (stars and dark
matter unchanged; P_th unchanged).
