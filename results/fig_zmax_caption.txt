Figure. The footprint-mean gas density, the quality of single-layer fits and the midplane alpha
as functions of height, to |z| = 1000 pc, the largest height at which the Edenhofer+23 map
('main' flavour, distance range 68.6-1248.1 pc) covers the full 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint (it does
so up to |z| = 1028 pc). RAW density, all footprint cells (HIM-flagged included), balanced
midplane z_mid = -11.9 pc (dotted line). (a) <n_H>_area in 4 pc bins of u = |z - z_mid| on each
side, plotted at signed z: the cube for |z| <= 750 pc (line) and a direct query of the map beyond
it (markers; 2 pc x,y grid over the footprint, 4 pc z steps, n_H = 1653 A'). The band and the
error bars are the error of log10 <n_H>_area: the standard deviation of log10 <n_H> over the 16
disjoint 250 x 250 pc squares of the footprint divided by sqrt(16). Coloured curves: exponential,
Gaussian and sech^2 layers centred on z_mid, free amplitude and scale height, fitted to the binned
log10 density of each side separately, shown for the fit range at that model's knee (solid within
it, dashed beyond). Light shading: |z - z_mid| <= 400 pc (the analysis box); thin vertical lines:
the cube edges, z = +-750 pc. (b) Reduced chi^2 = chi^2/(N_bins - 2) of the three fits versus the
fit range z_max (all bins with upper edge <= z_max), solid for the upper side (z > z_mid), dashed
for the lower side; markers at the knees, defined as the first z_max at or beyond the minimum of
reduced chi^2 over z_max <= 400 pc where reduced chi^2 exceeds twice that minimum. The vertical
line marks 750 pc. (c) The headline midplane <alpha_cell> = <W> <1/P_th> over the slab
|z - z_mid| <= 30 pc (as in Fig. 4) versus the integration height z_top: the density is set to
zero at |z| > z_top, and the gas self-gravity, the balanced midplane and the weight W are
recomputed from the truncated profile (stars and dark matter unchanged; P_th unchanged). Solid:
z_top <= 750 pc (cube only); dashed: z_top > 750 pc (cube plus map query). The shaded band is the
range of the knees in (b), 130-260 pc.

Knees (z_max, pc), upper / lower side: exponential 260 / 180, Gaussian 160 / 130, sech^2 190 / 130.
All 546 fits (91 fit ranges x 3 models x 2 sides) converged inside the parameter bounds. At z_max = 1000 pc the reduced chi^2 is
3.9 / 14.8 (exponential), 6.7 / 24.5 (Gaussian) and 6.0 / 22.1 (sech^2).
<alpha_cell> = 2.31 at z_top = 400 pc, 3.25 at 750 pc (the current fiducial) and 3.77 at
1000 pc. The area-averaged alpha_area_vol of the high-|z| tail diagnostic (Sun-centred slab
|z| <= 30 pc, <P_tot>_area / <P_th>_neutral,vol) is 1.65, 2.32 and 2.69 at the same heights.

Cube/query mismatch. At the cube's own 2 pc nodes, the map query gives a lower <n_H>_area than
the cube near the cube edge: query/cube = 0.89 (upper) and 0.78 (lower) at 740 <= |z| <= 750 pc,
0.91 and 0.84 over 600-750 pc, the visible steps at z = +-750 pc in (a). The ratio is the same with
and without the cube's orientation mapping and at 2 or 10 pc x,y sampling. It depends on
heliocentric distance on both sides (0.92-1.05 at 200-700 pc, 0.67-0.87 beyond 700-800 pc),
which points to a difference between the two renderings of the map: the cube was built from a 2
kpc sphere rendering, while the query uses the 1.25 kpc 'main' flavour. Nothing is rescaled.
