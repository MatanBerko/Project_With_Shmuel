Figure. Single- and two-component layer fits to the footprint-mean gas density, their fit
quality versus fit range, and the midplane alpha versus integration height, within the cube
(|z| <= 750 pc). RAW density, all footprint cells (HIM-flagged included), |x|, |y| <= 500 pc,
balanced midplane z_mid = -11.9 pc (dotted line). Left column: single component; right column:
two components.
(a), (b) <n_H>_area in 4 pc bins of u = |z - z_mid|, upper (z > z_mid) and lower (z < z_mid)
sides separately, plotted at signed z. The band is the error of log10 <n_H>_area: the standard
deviation of log10 <n_H> over the 16 disjoint 250 x 250 pc squares of the footprint divided by
sqrt(16). All profiles are centred on z_mid and fitted to the binned log10 density of each side
by least squares with these errors (scipy curve_fit, trf). Single component, free n0 and h:
exponential n0 exp(-u/h), Gaussian n0 exp(-u^2/2h^2), sech^2 n0 sech^2(u/h). Two components,
free n1, h1, n2, h2: the sum of two profiles of the same shape, n1 f(u/h1) + n2 f(u/h2), with
h2 = h1 + dh and dh > 0, so that h1 < h2. Bounds: n, n1, n2 in [1e-6, 1e3] cm^-3; h, h1 in
[1, 1e5] pc; dh in [1e-6, 1e5] pc. Two-component start values: h1 from the single fit at the same
fit range, h2 = 1000 pc, n2 = the binned profile at u = 600 pc, n1 = the first bin. The curves are
the best fits over the full range, all bins with upper edge <= 750 pc (u <= 748 pc on the upper
side; u <= 738 pc on the lower side, where the cube ends), drawn over the fitted bins; in (b) the
two components are drawn dotted in the same colour.
(c), (d) Reduced chi^2 versus the fit range z_max (all bins with upper edge <= z_max),
z_max = 100-750 pc in 10 pc steps; solid for the upper side, dashed for the lower side.
Reduced chi^2 = chi^2/(N_bins - 2) for one component and chi^2/(N_bins - 4) for two. In (d) a
two-component fit is drawn only where it is constrained: converged, no parameter at a bound, and
a relative 1-sigma error on h2 below 50% (errors from the fit covariance;
sigma_h2^2 = var h1 + var dh + 2 cov(h1, dh)).
(e) The headline midplane <alpha_cell> = <W> <1/P_th> over the slab |z - z_mid| <= 30 pc (as in
Fig. 4) versus the integration height z_top: the density is set to zero at |z| > z_top, and the
gas self-gravity, the balanced midplane and the weight W are recomputed from the truncated
profile (stars and dark matter unchanged; P_th unchanged).
