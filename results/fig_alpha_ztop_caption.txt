Figure. The vertical profiles of Fig. 4 for four integration heights z_top = 400, 500, 600 and
750 pc (light to dark; 750 pc, thick, is the fiducial of Fig. 4), in Fig. 4's 4 pc bins of z over
the 1 kpc x 1 kpc footprint (RAW density). For each z_top the footprint-mean density is set to
zero at |z| > z_top, and the gas weight W(z) = the integral of rho g from z to the edge of the
column is recomputed from the truncated profile: footprint-mean gas self-gravity from the
truncated profile, Guo+20 stars and dark matter, and the balanced midplane re-found for that
profile (z_mid = -17.4, -15.6, -13.9 and -11.9 pc; the dotted line marks the fiducial one).
(a) The area-averaged weight <W> per bin for each z_top, and the harmonic-mean thermal pressure
<1/P_th>^-1 over the non-HIM cells of the bin (black; the same for every z_top). (b) The headline
<alpha_cell> = <W> <1/P_th> per bin for each z_top, volume-weighted; the shaded band is the
15th-85th percentile range of the per-cell alpha_cell = <W>/P_th for z_top = 750 pc; the grey
line marks alpha = 1. (c) The same profiles divided by each z_top's midplane value, <alpha_cell>
over the slab |z - z_mid| <= 30 pc (with that z_top's z_mid). For z_top = 750 pc every bin
reproduces Fig. 4 to 1e-10.

Midplane slab <alpha_cell> = 2.31, 2.56, 2.84 and 3.25 for z_top = 400, 500, 600 and 750 pc.
